## Metareview

The paper describes the creation of a Chinese dictionary with an LLM. The dictionary was constructed as a merge of 7 community dictionaries and it is translated into 18 languages.

## Summary of Reasons to Publish

The main reason to publish on which all reviewers agree is that the paper presents a very useful resource. The reviewers also appreciated that the approach is reproducible and affordable.

## Summary of Suggested Revisions

Probably the biggest concern of this paper is relying on a single closed-source model. So very likely the paper would benefit from finding a suitable open-weight model that achieves comparable results.

The reviewers were also concerned about the scientific novelty of the paper.

The reviewers missed more detailed error analysis and were commenting on the fact that pivoting through English for some languages might yield inaccurate results.

The reviewers agree that human evaluation and more automatic metrics would further improve the paper.
